# ⚖️ Disclaimer — responsible use

**Plain version:** Clone Anything is for learning, inspiration, and prototyping. Build a copy to study how a great site is built, to practice, to pitch an idea — then make it *yours* before it goes anywhere public.

## ✅ What it's for

- **Learning** — seeing how a polished app is designed and structured
- **Prototyping** — starting your own product from a proven layout instead of a blank page
- **Reskinning with permission** — cloning your own product, your client's site (with their OK), or open-source projects (respecting their license)
- **Comparing** — studying a competitor's flows to understand what makes them work

## ❌ What it's not for

- **Passing a clone off as someone else's work**, or tricking users into thinking they're on the real site (phishing, impersonation)
- **Publishing a clone with someone else's logo, brand name, copyrighted images, or content** — the skill uses neutral placeholders by default; keep them, or swap in assets you own or licensed
- **Deceiving** anyone — investors, users, reviewers — with a copy that pretends to be a real, working service it isn't
- Anything **illegal** in your jurisdiction

## Who's responsible

**You are.** The skill gives you a faithful copy of a *look and feel*; what you build on top of it and where you put it is your call and your responsibility. The project and its contributors are not liable for how the output is used. The software is provided as-is, with no warranty (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

**One more honest note:** a clone's fidelity scores measure *visual* similarity — a high score never means "safe to publish." Check trademarks, copyrights, and your target site's terms yourself before anything goes public.
